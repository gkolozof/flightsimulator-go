Frequently Asked Questions

What flight simulator models do you offer?

Professional Cessna 172 and Cessna 152 Magic Full Embedded (Big and Mini), ATC Simulator, Drone Simulator and individual cockpit components.

What are the purchase costs?

Full-embedded professional units start from approximately €65.000–€70.000. ATC from €350.000. Contact us for a formal quotation.

How does installation and technical support work?

Turnkey delivery and installation support. Typical window 2–3 months. Ongoing remote monitoring and maintenance available.

Are there studies proving effectiveness?

Yes. Research from the University of Illinois indicates approximately 40% fewer errors in first real flight hours after simulator training.

Is the system fully automatic?

Yes. Magic Full Embedded is fully automatic and controlled via iPad/iPhone.
